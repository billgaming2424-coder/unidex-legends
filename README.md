# 🌌 UniMon Legends (UniDex Legends)

An open-ended, web-based multiversal monster collecting and management RPG built with vanilla web technologies, Node.js, and real-time Socket.IO multiplayer networking.

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

Step into the shoes of a newly licensed Multiverse Trainer in the nexus world of **Aetheria**. Explore diverse dimensional portals, battle wild entities, mine rare minerals in underground caverns, build your roster of recruits, and team up with other trainers in real-time online lobbies.

### Key Features

* **📖 Massive 1–2000 UniDex:** Explore, catalog, and hunt across thousands of multiversal companion entries with live caught badges and quick-encounter triggers.
* **🌐 Real-Time Multiplayer Hub:** Connect with friends in shared timeline lobbies, track live online trainers, duel in PvP matches, and coordinate party attacks in Co-Op Raid Boss battles.
* **🗺️ Biome Exploration & Combat:** Travel between regions—from the Whispering Forest to the Obsidian Volcano—to fight enemies in turn-based combat.
* **⛏️ Stardew-Style Mines:** Delve into deep cavern floors, smash rocks for rare Copper Ores and Diamonds, and manage your stamina.
* **🏪 Market District & NPCs:** Trade minerals with Doug, purchase combat gear and supplies from Don, or visit Fr3xi0us's Black Market for rare items.
* **🎰 Multiverse Lucky Wheel:** Risk daily funds on the spinning wheel for bonus loot, diamonds, Pokéballs, and stamina boosts.
* **🌱 Greenhouse & Lifestyle:** Upgrade and decorate your private penthouse suite, manage crops in the greenhouse, and interact with your recruited companions.
* **🔒 Premium DLC Portals:** Enter secure dimensional passcodes to access exclusive themed realms (Goth IHOP, Anime Maid Café, Femboy Hooters, and seasonal holiday rifts).
* **🎮 Native Gamepad Support:** Built-in Gamepad API engine allowing full navigation and selection using a controller.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

* **Frontend:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3 animations, and asynchronous JavaScript (ES6+).
* **Backend:** Node.js & Express.
* **Networking:** Socket.IO for real-time room communication, trainer presence tracking, and raid coordination.
* **Storage:** Browser `localStorage` multi-slot save architecture with automated state synchronization.
