## 🎮 Game Overview

Step into the nexus world of **Aetheria** as a newly licensed Multiverse Trainer. Explore dimensional rifts, recruit allies, mine cavern depths, trade with local merchants, and team up with friends in real-time online lobbies.

### Core Features

* **📖 Complete 1–2000 UniDex:** Discover, catalog, and recruit hundreds of multiversal entities with live caught badges and direct-catch hunt triggers.
* **🌐 Live Multiplayer Hub:** Join shared rooms with friends, see who is active across dimensions, battle in PvP duels, and team up against Co-Op Raid Bosses.
* **🗺️ Dynamic Biome Exploration:** Venture into the Whispering Forest, Sunscorched Desert, and Obsidian Volcano for turn-based combat encounters and unique loot.
* **⛏️ Stardew-Style Caverns:** Smash rocks, manage daily stamina, and mine valuable Copper Ores and Diamonds.
* **🏪 Market District & NPCs:** Trade minerals at Doug's Ore Exchange, stock up at Don's General Store, or browse Fr3xi0us's Black Market.
* **🎰 Multiverse Lucky Wheel:** Spin daily for bonus cash, rare gems, extra Pokéballs, and stamina refills.
* **🌱 Greenhouse & Lifestyle:** Upgrade to a luxury penthouse, decorate your suite, and tend crops in the hydroponic greenhouse.
* **🔒 Themed & Holiday Portals:** Unlock secret passcode portals including Goth IHOP, Anime Maid Café, and seasonal holiday rifts.
* **🎮 Controller Support:** Built-in Gamepad API navigation for smooth controller gameplay.
